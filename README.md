# Robotics Hardware Design

A collection of my robotics hardware design work, covering **CAD, mechanical design, 3D printing, mechanisms, and PCB design**.

This repository documents my learning and development of practical hardware-design skills for building robotic systems.

## Areas Covered

* **CAD & Mechanical Design**

  * Fusion 360
  * Mechanical components
  * Robot mounts and brackets
  * Enclosures and chassis
  * Shafts, bearings, and joints
  * Mechanisms and linkages
  * Gear designs

* **3D Printing**

  * 3D-printable mechanical parts
  * STL/3MF preparation
  * Prototype fabrication
  * Fit and tolerance testing
  * Design iterations

* **PCB Design**

  * KiCad
  * Schematic design
  * Component and footprint selection
  * PCB layout
  * Routing
  * Design Rule Checking (DRC)
  * 3D PCB visualization
  * Gerber generation

## Design Process

I follow an iterative engineering workflow:

```text
Idea
  ↓
Design
  ↓
CAD / Schematic
  ↓
Prototype
  ↓
3D Print / PCB Fabrication
  ↓
Test
  ↓
Identify Problems
  ↓
Redesign
  ↓
Version 2 / Improved Prototype
```

The goal is not only to create designs that look correct digitally, but to **build, test, evaluate, and improve them**.
