# PCB Design

This folder contains my **PCB design and electronics hardware projects** developed using **KiCad**.

The work here documents my progression from basic PCB-design practice to designing custom boards for robotics and embedded systems.

## What I'm Learning

* Circuit schematic design
* Component selection
* Datasheets and electrical specifications
* Footprint selection
* PCB layout
* Through-hole (THT) and SMD components
* Routing and trace placement
* Ground and power connections
* Vias and copper zones
* Design Rule Checking (DRC)
* 3D PCB visualization
* Gerber generation
* PCB fabrication and testing

## Tools

* **KiCad** — schematic and PCB design
* **Datasheets** — component specifications and footprint verification
